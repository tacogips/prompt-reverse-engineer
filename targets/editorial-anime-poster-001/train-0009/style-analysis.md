## Direct observations from style truth

### Medium and rendering

- Polished editorial anime illustration with precise drawing over opaque poster-print fields.
- Forms are organized into flat, clean planes with restrained two- to three-step cel shading; highlights are selective and sharp rather than airbrushed.
- Dark navy ink contains the silhouette and selected interior details, alternating firm contour strokes with very fine tapered filaments.

### Palette and tonal behavior

- A deliberately narrow screen-print palette combines a warm off-white paper ground, deep navy anchor, saturated teal, coral, yellow, and sparse muted-gold marks.
- Broad color is opaque and crisply bounded; tonal depth comes from value grouping and small accent planes rather than continuous photographic gradients.
- Skin-like planes are light and warm against navy contours, with a restrained warm shadow band and minimal bright highlight. This is a relative plane treatment, not a fixed skin color.
- Hair-like masses are grouped as a very dark ink base, then separated by cool teal/cyan and warm coral accent strands or rim planes. The color relationship describes mass grouping, not hairstyle or literal hair color.
- Clothing-like areas are reduced to interlocking, limited-palette geometric fabric planes: a dark anchor, pale planes, and selected teal/coral/yellow facets, bounded by decisive ink seams and small gold marks. This describes stylization, not a particular garment.

### Line, shape, and mark making

- Variable-weight navy contours are confident and tapered, especially along organic edges; interior lines are sparse and delicate.
- Adaptable poster support language uses oversized circles, clipped panels, vertical rules, diagonals, and curved sweeps, but their literal positions are not reusable evidence.
- Fine gold lines, dots, connectors, and small star-like nodes form a secondary ornamental layer.
- Dry-brush sweeps, occasional scraped edges, flecks, and small speckles introduce controlled handmade energy.

### Texture and material

- The off-white paper ground remains visible between fields.
- Opaque printed regions have subtle grain, pinprick flecks, irregular overprint boundaries, and locally dry-brushed interruptions.
- Texture is sparse and legibility-preserving, never a heavy distressed overlay.

### Abstract graphic language and conditional lettering

- The transferable hierarchy is broad opaque support fields, source-controlled focal content, then sparse fine ornament, with asymmetric balance and generous negative-space rhythm.
- Where lettering is used, its reusable treatment is a small, high-contrast editorial display accent: light ink against a dark or saturated field, elegant handwritten/display-like stroke quality, and a restrained supporting line of small type. Its hierarchy and placement are adaptable; its wording, script, and exact location are not.
- Optional exact text should be the sole authorization for lettering. It may use a clean high-contrast display treatment with delicate ink/overprint character and small dot/line ornaments, without inventing further words or pseudo-text.
