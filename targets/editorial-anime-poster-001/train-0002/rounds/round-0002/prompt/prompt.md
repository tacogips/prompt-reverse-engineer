# Rationale

The round-0001 winner preserves the intended man, the poster composition, palette, and technical cleanliness, but its largest weighted loss is style: the hair and facial linework are too smooth and the garment lacks the reference’s fine layered graphic detail. The compact short-hair silhouette and identity clauses are made explicit to prevent the known hair-volume regression.
