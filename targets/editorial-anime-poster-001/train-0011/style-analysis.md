# Style analysis — direct observations and transferable inference

## Evidence scope

- Style truth was inspected only as immutable analysis and judge evidence.
- Training source was inspected only to establish the content-preservation boundary. Its observed identity, apparel, markings, pose, setting, and incidental interface graphics are excluded from the reusable prompt.
- Historical train-0010 evidence is used as a prior hypothesis, not as a substitute for the current observations or a source of held-out inputs.

## Direct style-truth observations

### Medium and rendering

The image is a polished editorial anime poster illustration rather than photography. Forms are built from large opaque color regions and disciplined two-to-three-step cel-shaded planes. Facial and material transitions are graphic and bounded, with crisp printed separation rather than airbrushed realism. The focal form uses precise dark ink definition plus selected fine internal detail.

### Palette and tonal behavior

A warm off-white paper field carries deep navy anchors, saturated cyan-teal, coral-red, and sunny yellow support colors. Muted metallic-gold is reserved for small accent lines and dots. Contrast comes from adjacent flat value masses; gradients are shallow and controlled. Skin-like areas use a luminous light plane, restrained warm shadow bands, narrow highlights, and selective navy containment. Hair-like areas remain a dark unified mass, then receive a few cool cyan and warm coral value accents. Garment-like areas use interlocking pale, navy, teal, coral, yellow, and muted-gold facets, but their reading depends on dark seam/edge anchors and a light print texture.

### Line and shape language

Contours are deep navy, variable-weight, and confidently tapered. Fine loose strokes occasionally extend past a contour, giving energetic ink motion without becoming sketchy. Abstract support forms are adaptable: large cropped discs, solid blocks, vertical rules, brush sweeps, small dots, thin arcs, and connected orbit-like lines. These are a vocabulary, not a fixed layout.

### Texture, material, and mark making

The paper ground remains visible. Screen-print speckling, minute flecks, occasional broken overprint edges, and dry-brush interruptions make opaque fields tactile. Marks are sparse enough to preserve facial and garment legibility; they read as imperfect print and paint, not wear, dirt, or random noise.

### Abstract graphic vocabulary and conditional lettering

The hierarchy is editorial and asymmetric: large opaque support fields establish a rhythm, source-controlled content remains primary, then fine gold/ink orbit lines and dots add a final ornamental layer. Visible display lettering is a single high-contrast calligraphic/editorial accent with elegant thin strokes. Its transferable grammar is readable display ink, restrained hierarchy, adaptive orientation, and placement in unused negative space or an abstract support field. The teacher wording, language, script, exact position, and related symbols are excluded.

## Reusable inference

Use a source-only editorial-anime poster transformation with limited warm-paper/navy/teal/coral/yellow/gold print relationships, opaque cel-shaded planes, variable navy ink, and restrained screen-print texture. Treat skin, hair, and clothing relatively: preserve literal source identity and colors while converting value grouping, highlight/shadow design, contours, and print-facet treatment. Clothing must retain source garment silhouette, construction, markings, and logos; it may gain only restrained source-relative value facets, seam anchoring, and subtle overprint texture. Render no lettering unless exact caller text is supplied.

## Excluded teacher content

Do not transfer any teacher subject, facial identity, hair arrangement, accessories, garment design, readable words, language, pose, crop, exact field coordinates, object inventory, scene, or brushstroke trajectory. These exclusions also apply to the training source: its observed person, clothing, logo, pose, setting, and interface artifacts are evidence for source fidelity only, never prompt content.
